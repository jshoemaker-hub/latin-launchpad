About

Focused Latin practice for every class day.

Latin Launchpad is an independent educational resource that helps students in grades 3–8 build confidence with Latin vocabulary, grammar, phrases, and culture.

Why it exists

Latin is easiest to retain when students can practice in short, repeatable sessions. Latin Launchpad brings lessons, quick checks, study tools, printable activities, and progress tracking together in one approachable place.

What students can do

  • Work through grade-level vocabulary and grammar lessons.
  • Use flashcards, quizzes, puzzles, and a site-wide dictionary.
  • Explore common Latin phrases, stories, and cultural notes.
  • Track lesson completion, mastery, points, and badges.

Our approach

The site is designed as a practice companion, not a replacement for a teacher, curriculum, or individualized academic guidance. Content is organized for steady skill-building and can be used at home, in a classroom, or alongside another Latin program.

Questions or corrections are welcome.

Latin is a rich subject, and careful feedback makes the resource better. Please use the contact page to report an issue or suggest an improvement.
